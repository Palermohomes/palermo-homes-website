*About Palermo Homes · Kelowna, BC*

Fifteen-plus years designing and building custom homes and renovations across Kelowna, West Kelowna, Lake Country, and the Okanagan Valley.

*Overview*

## Custom homes and renovations across Kelowna, the Okanagan, and Lower Mainland.

Palermo Homes is a custom home builder in Kelowna, BC. We build custom homes, renovations, and take on general contracting across Kelowna, West Kelowna, Lake Country, Peachland, and the Okanagan Valley.

*Our approach*

## Three ideas that shape every home we build.

- 01**Accountability**One team, one contract, one point of contact. We self-perform the trades that matter most and coordinate the rest as your general contractor, so quality stays in-house.
- 02**Transparency**Line-item pricing from the start. Weekly progress reporting with photos and milestones. No surprises at draw stages or hand-over.
- 03**Long-term value**Commercial-grade construction paired with design that respects the Kelowna resale market. Homes that hold their look and their value for decades.

*Meet the owner*

## Jas Sidhu, owner of Palermo Homes.

Jas has led Palermo Homes for over 15 years, personally overseeing every custom home build from first design meeting to final walkthrough. He takes the first call on every new project.

His approach is grounded in fixed-price contracts, documented change orders, and senior on-site project management on every build. No cost-plus surprises, no handoffs to junior PMs stretched across six jobs.
