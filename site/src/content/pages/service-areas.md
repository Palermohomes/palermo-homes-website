*Where we build*

## Rooted in Kelowna. Building across the Okanagan and beyond.

Palermo Homes is a custom home builder headquartered in Kelowna, BC (BC Housing License 42812, BC Corporate Registration BC1017682). We build across the Central Okanagan including Kelowna, West Kelowna, Lake Country, and Peachland, plus Vernon, Penticton, and select Lower Mainland communities for returning clients.

Fifteen-plus years in the Okanagan and Fraser Valley means we have navigated the permit rhythms, geotechnical conditions, and architectural design review boards in every city on this page, from Upper Mission hillsides and Wilden hilltop lots to Naramata Bench vineyard-view parcels and Eagle Mountain in Abbotsford.

*Why clients across the Okanagan hire Palermo*

## One team. Seven cities. Fifteen years of local build knowledge.

### Hyperlocal permit fluency

Every municipality in our service area has its own permit process, review timelines, and inspectors. We know the City of Kelowna, City of West Kelowna, District of Lake Country, District of Peachland, Regional District of Central Okanagan, City of Vernon, City of Penticton, and the City of Abbotsford processes directly, so permits move without surprises.

### Hillside and lakefront experience

Most Central Okanagan custom builds involve hillside geotech, foreshore setbacks, or both. We have delivered custom homes on Upper Mission benches, Lakeview Heights slopes above Okanagan Lake, Naramata Bench vineyard terraces, and Lakestone lakefront lots. The structural, drainage, and siting playbooks are already built.

### One team from design to hand-over

Our owner, Jas Sidhu, is directly involved on every active build in every city we work in. Clients get one point of contact, one fixed-price contract, and one project team, whether the lot is in Kelowna, Vernon, or Abbotsford.
