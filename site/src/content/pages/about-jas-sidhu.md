## About Jas

Jas Sidhu has led Palermo Homes for over 15 years, building custom homes and whole-home renovations across Kelowna, West Kelowna, Lake Country, Peachland, Penticton, Vernon, and the Lower Mainland.

His approach is grounded in three things: fixed-price contracts with transparent line-item budgeting, documented change orders so clients are never surprised, and senior on-site project management on every build. The result is a track record of homes delivered on budget, on schedule, and with the kind of finish quality that gets referenced in neighbourhood architectural reviews.

## How Jas builds

Every Palermo Homes project runs on the same three operating principles. These are the things Jas insists on with every client before a contract is signed.

### Fixed-price contracts, not cost-plus

Jas does not run cost-plus builds. Every Palermo contract is fixed-price with documented allowances for finishes. Clients know their number before construction starts, and the number does not creep because the builder is incentivized to control cost.

### Senior on-site project management

Jas is personally involved in every project. Clients do not get handed off to a junior PM running six other jobs. Decisions get made, not deferred.

### Documented change orders

If something changes during construction, it gets priced, written up, and signed before work starts. No verbal handshakes that turn into disputes.

If you want the full version of this philosophy applied to choosing any Kelowna builder, Jas wrote the [builder selection guide](/guides/how-to-choose-custom-home-builder-kelowna/) that walks through the seven verification steps every homeowner should run before signing anything.
