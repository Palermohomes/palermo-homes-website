  - {"q":"What warranty do you offer on new custom homes?","a":"Every Palermo custom home is covered by British Columbia's mandatory 2-5-10 New Home Warranty program. The coverage breaks down as: 2 years for labour and materials (workmanship defects), 5 years for the building envelope (water penetration, moisture protection), and 10 years for major structural defects (load-bearing elements, foundation). This is the highest standard warranty available for new construction in Canada. Our warranty is backed by a third-party provider registered with BC Housing, not by Palermo alone, so coverage is enforceable even if a builder went out of business."}
