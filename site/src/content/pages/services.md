*Overview*

## A full-service custom home builder serving Kelowna and the Okanagan since 2014.

Palermo Homes Ltd. is a BC Housing Licensed Residential Builder (License 42812) running three service lines under one roof: custom home design and construction, whole-home renovations, and project management on general contracting work. Every project is handled by in-house staff from first design meeting through final walk-through, which keeps the finish standard consistent whether we are building a lakefront custom home on Cabernet Way or renovating a 1980s split-level in Glenmore.

Our primary service area is the Central Okanagan (Kelowna, West Kelowna, Lake Country, Peachland), with active work in Vernon and Penticton and select Fraser Valley and Lower Mainland projects in Abbotsford. Pricing is transparent: we offer both fixed-price and cost-plus contract structures and walk through both before signing. Every new build carries the BC 2-5-10 New Home Warranty.
