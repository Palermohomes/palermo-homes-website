## Recognized across BC's custom home industry.

Palermo Homes is an award-winning custom home builder based in Kelowna, BC. Recognized by the Canadian Home Builders' Association (CHBA) Fraser Valley Housing Awards of Excellence for custom home construction and design across Kelowna, West Kelowna, the Okanagan Valley, and the Lower Mainland.

*Featured recognition*

## 4th Annual Fraser Valley Housing Awards of Excellence.

Feb 22, 2020 · Airside Event Spaces, Abbotsford BC

- Winner Best Master Suite
- Top 3 Finalist Custom Home Builder of the Year
- Top 3 Finalist Best Custom Home ($1M-$1.5M)
- Top 3 Finalist Best Kitchen
- Top 3 Finalist Best Outdoor Living Space
