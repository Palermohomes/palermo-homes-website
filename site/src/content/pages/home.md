## A licensed custom home builder in Kelowna and West Kelowna with 15+ years of local experience.

Palermo Homes is a BC Housing Licensed Residential Builder (License 42812, Corporate Registration BC1017682) based in West Kelowna. We design and build custom homes and whole-home renovations across the Central Okanagan, with deep working knowledge of local lot conditions, municipal permit processes, and the trades who deliver quality on every build.

### Local Okanagan expertise

As a [custom home builder in Kelowna](/) and [West Kelowna custom home builder](/service-areas/west-kelowna/) based in the valley, we build regularly in Upper Mission, Kettle Valley, Wilden, Lakeview Heights, McKinley Beach, Predator Ridge, and across the Central Okanagan hillside lots that most of our clients are drawn to.

Hillside and lakeview sites, Development Permit areas, and architectural guideline communities all require specific experience. [Our hillside builds guide](/guides/hillside-custom-home-kelowna/) covers what makes these projects different.

### Licensed, insured, and warranted

Every Palermo Homes custom build is registered under BC Housing License 42812 and covered by the mandatory 2-5-10 New Home Warranty. We work on fixed-price contracts with transparent line-item budgeting, documented change orders, and senior on-site project management on every job.

How contracts should be structured, and why fixed-price protects you, is covered in [our contracts guide](/guides/fixed-price-vs-cost-plus-custom-home-kelowna/).

### Transparent process and portfolio

Our 8-phase custom home process runs from first consultation to move-in with defined deliverables and timelines at each stage. We publish real cost ranges, timeline expectations, and permit specifics so clients plan with real numbers from day one.

Palermo Homes serves Kelowna clients from our West Kelowna base, 10 minutes across the W.R. Bennett Bridge.
