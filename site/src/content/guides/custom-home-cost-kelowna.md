A complete cost breakdown for custom homes in Kelowna and the Okanagan, including per-square-foot pricing, hidden costs, hillside build premiums, and how to budget realistically.

Published September 2026 · By [Jas Sidhu](/about/jas-sidhu/), Owner of Palermo Homes · Custom Home Builder in West Kelowna, BC

The typical cost to build a custom home in Kelowna in 2026 ranges from $500 to $900 per square foot for above-grade finished space, plus $200 to $350 per square foot for finished basement space. A standard 3,000 square foot custom home in Kelowna typically costs between $1.5 million and $2.7 million for the home alone, not including land.

## Cost breakdown by build tier

Custom home construction costs in Kelowna fall into three broad tiers based on finishes, architectural complexity, and site conditions.

### Entry-level custom builds: $500 to $600 per square foot

Standard finishes, straightforward architecture, flat lots. A 3,000 square foot entry-level custom home in Kelowna typically costs $1.5 million to $1.8 million.

Included at this tier:

- Standard cabinets and countertops
- Standard flooring (carpet, laminate, some tile)
- Basic lighting packages
- Standard windows and doors
- Simple rooflines

### Mid-range custom homes: $650 to $800 per square foot

Premium finishes, engineered hardwood, quartz counters, custom cabinetry, moderate architectural complexity. A 3,000 square foot mid-range custom home in Kelowna typically costs $1.95 million to $2.4 million.

Included at this tier:

- Custom cabinetry throughout
- Engineered hardwood flooring
- Quartz or granite countertops
- Premium plumbing fixtures
- Higher-end lighting
- Better window packages

### High-end custom builds: $850 to $1,200+ per square foot

Imported materials, complex geometry, specialty systems, wine rooms, home theatres, indoor pools, or bespoke millwork. A 3,000 square foot high-end custom home in Kelowna typically costs $2.55 million to $3.6 million or more.

Included at this tier:

- Fully custom millwork
- Imported stone, tile, and hardwood
- Complex architectural features
- Smart home automation
- Specialty rooms (wine cellars, theatres, gyms)
- Premium appliance packages

## Hillside and waterfront build premiums

Building on a hillside lot in Kelowna adds $100,000 to $400,000 over a comparable flat lot. This applies to popular neighborhoods including [Upper Mission](/neighborhoods/upper-mission/), [Kettle Valley](/neighborhoods/upper-mission/), McKinley Beach, [Lakeview Heights](/neighborhoods/lakeview-heights/) in West Kelowna, and [Predator Ridge](/neighborhoods/predator-ridge/) near Vernon.

Hillside premium costs include:

- Retaining walls and site engineering
- Deep pilings for hillside foundations
- Drainage systems
- Geotechnical assessments
- Additional excavation
- Access road construction

Waterfront lots on Okanagan Lake, Kalamalka Lake, or Skaha Lake add similar premiums due to shoreline setback requirements, environmental assessments, and specialty foundation work.

## What is the typical cost to build a 2,000 square foot house in BC?

A 2,000 square foot custom home in Kelowna typically costs $1 million to $1.8 million for the home alone. This assumes above-grade finished space at $500 to $900 per square foot, no finished basement included, a flat or gently sloped lot, and mid-range finishes.

Add $400,000 to $700,000 for a comparable finished basement, and $100,000 to $400,000 for hillside site conditions.

## What is the typical cost to build a 3,000 square foot house in BC?

A 3,000 square foot custom home in Kelowna typically costs $1.5 million to $2.7 million for the home alone. Add $600,000 to $1.05 million if you finish the basement.

## What is the typical cost to build a 4,000 square foot house in BC?

A 4,000 square foot custom home in Kelowna typically costs $2 million to $3.6 million for the home alone. Add $800,000 to $1.4 million for a finished basement, common at this size.

## How long does it take to build a custom home in Kelowna?

A typical custom home in Kelowna takes 14 to 20 months from your first design meeting to move-in:

- 3 to 5 months for architectural design and interior selections
- 2 to 3 months for permits and geotechnical assessment (longer on hillside lots)
- 9 to 12 months for actual construction

Hillside builds and homes with specialty features often add 4 to 8 weeks to the total timeline.

## Costs most Kelowna builders don't include upfront

The published per-square-foot rate is only part of the story. Real total budgets for custom home construction in Kelowna also need to account for:

**Land costs:** $500,000 to $2.5 million for a Central Okanagan lot. Waterfront and hillside lots cost significantly more.

**Design fees:** $30,000 to $80,000 for architectural design. Some builders include this in the per-square-foot price; others charge separately.

**Permits and development cost charges:** $15,000 to $50,000 depending on the city (Kelowna, West Kelowna, Lake Country, Peachland, Penticton, Vernon all have different fee structures).

**Landscaping and driveway:** $30,000 to $150,000 depending on scope.

**GST:** 5% on the finished home value (this is significant on a $2 million home).

**Contingency:** 10 to 15% of construction cost is standard for unforeseen conditions.

## How to budget realistically

Add up the following to get a realistic total budget for a custom home in Kelowna:

- Land cost
- Design and permits ($50,000 to $130,000)
- Construction cost (square footage times per-square-foot rate)
- Hillside/waterfront premium if applicable
- Landscaping ($50,000+)
- GST (5% of home value)
- 10 to 15% contingency

For a mid-range 3,000 square foot custom home on a flat lot in Kelowna, this typically totals $2.5 million to $3.5 million all-in, including land.

## Why per-square-foot rates alone are misleading

Different builders include different things in their per-square-foot rate. Some quote a "shell" price that excludes cabinets, appliances, and finishes. Others quote a fully finished, turnkey price. When comparing bids from Kelowna custom home builders, always ask what is and is not included.

A $600 per square foot quote that excludes finishes may end up costing more than a $750 per square foot quote that includes everything.

Custom home cost expectations should always be discussed in the context of your specific lot, design, and finish preferences. The ranges in this article reflect current 2026 market conditions in the Central Okanagan.
