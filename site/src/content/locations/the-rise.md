*Overview*

## Custom home builder in The Rise, Vernon.

Palermo Homes is a licensed BC Housing custom home builder that brings in-house cabinetry, custom finish carpentry, envelope discipline, and fixed-price contracts to every project. The Rise is a hillside golf-course community and view-corridor design is what these lots demand.

We serve The Rise from our Central Okanagan operation. Site visits and design meetings are a scheduled trip and we price that reality transparently. Bring us your lot survey and we walk the property with you before you commit.

The Rise is a master-planned hillside community by Okanagan Hills Development Corp. on the west side of Vernon, adjacent to Turtle Mountain. Anchored by an 18-hole Fred Couples signature golf course. Community includes townhomes, single-family lots, and estate custom home sites capturing panoramic Okanagan Lake, valley, and mountain views. Amenities include high-end dining and year-round virtual golf. Schools are Vernon School District 22 (SD22). Downtown Vernon 10 min east via Highway 97, Kalamalka Lake 20 min east, Silver Star Mountain Resort 40 min northeast for winter skiing, Kelowna 45 min south, Kelowna International Airport (YLW) 40 min south.

*Why Palermo*

## Why The Rise clients hire Palermo.

- 01**Golf-course lot design experience**The Rise custom homes orient around the Fred Couples course and Okanagan Lake view corridor. We design floor plans that capture golf-course frontage and view corridors while managing privacy and light.
- 02**Hillside and slope experience**The Rise lots range from gently sloping to steep hillside cuts. Retaining, drainage, foundation stepping, and structural engineering matter here. We've been doing hillside custom work across the Okanagan for years.
- 03**City of Vernon permits**The Rise sits inside the City of Vernon. City permits, inspections, and servicing coordination run differently than the Regional District or District of Coldstream. We know the process.
- 04**Community design coordination**The Rise has phase-specific design covenants that vary by release. We check every lot for registered covenants at the design stage and manage the review package on your behalf.
- 05**Same finish standard as our Kelowna work**In-house cabinetry, custom finish carpentry, envelope details that hold up. The Rise homes carry high market value because the views and golf-course setting are premium, and we match the level of build the setting deserves.
